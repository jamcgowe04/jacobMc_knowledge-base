# Buying Records Online

Online shopping has transformed record collecting by giving collectors access to albums from sellers around the world. While this provides an incredible selection, it also requires buyers to carefully evaluate listings before making a purchase.

When shopping online, collectors should examine photographs, read descriptions thoroughly, and review the seller's grading practices. Checking customer feedback and return policies can also reduce the risk of receiving records that differ from their descriptions.

Records should be packaged securely using sturdy mailers and protective inserts to prevent damage during transit. Buyers should also factor shipping costs into the total purchase price, especially when ordering internationally.

Although buying online may involve more research than shopping locally, it allows collectors to locate rare pressings, compare prices, and expand their collections beyond what is available in their area.

### Before Purchasing Online

- Read the full description.
- Check record and jacket grades.
- Review seller ratings.
- Examine photos carefully.
- Confirm shipping costs and return policies.
