# Where to Buy Records

One of the most exciting aspects of record collecting is discovering new places to find vinyl. Records can be purchased from local record stores, online marketplaces, thrift shops, antique stores, garage sales, record fairs, and directly from artists or independent record labels. Each source offers unique advantages depending on a collector's goals and budget.

Local record stores often provide knowledgeable staff, carefully graded records, and opportunities to discover music that may not appear online. Shopping locally also supports small businesses and allows collectors to inspect records before making a purchase. Online marketplaces provide access to an enormous selection of albums from around the world, making them useful for finding rare releases or completing collections.

Collectors should compare prices across multiple sources and inspect a record's condition whenever possible. Taking time to research before purchasing helps avoid overpaying and increases the chances of finding high-quality records.

## Popular Places to Buy
- Local record stores
- Record fairs
- Thrift stores
- Antique shops
- Estate and garage sales
- Online marketplaces
- Artist websites
