---
title: Storing Records
date: 2026-07-25
---
___

Proper storage is essential for maintaining the condition of a vinyl collection. Records should always be stored vertically, similar to books on a shelf. Stacking records horizontally places excessive weight on those at the bottom and may cause warping over time.

Collectors should keep records in a cool, dry environment away from direct sunlight, excessive humidity, and heat sources. High temperatures can soften vinyl, while moisture may damage album jackets and encourage mold growth. Many Collectors also replace paper inner sleeves with anti-static sleeves to reduce dust and minimize scratches.

Outer protective sleeves help preserve album artwork by preventing shelf wear, fingerprints, and fading. Organizing records by artists, genre, or alphabetically also makes a collection easier to browse and reduces unnecessary handling. 

| Do                                | Don't                    |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Store records vertically          | Stack records flat       |
| Keep in a climate-controlled room | Store heaters or windows |
| Organize shelves                  | Overcrowd shelves        |
