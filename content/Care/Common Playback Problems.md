# Common Playback Problems

Even well-maintained records occasionally experience playback issues. Some of the most common problems include skipping, excessive surface noise, distortion, and static electricity. Understanding the causes of these issues allows collectors to troubleshoot problems before assuming a record is permanently damaged. 

Skipping often results from a dirty record, a worn stylus, or an improperly balanced tonearm. Pops and clicks are usually caused by dust trapped in the grooves, while static buildup may attract additional debris during playback.

Routine maintenance, including cleaning records, replacing worn styli, and checking turntable setup, resolves  many playback problems. However, scratches and groove damage are permanent and cannot usually be repaired. Learning the difference between maintenance issues and permanent damage helps collectors take better care of their collection.

Troubleshooting Guide
###### **Problem**                             **Possible Cause**
___
Skipping                              Dirty Record
___
Pops & Clicks                      Dust in grooves
___
Distortion                            Damaged stylus or record wear
___
Static                                   Dry air or improper sleeves
