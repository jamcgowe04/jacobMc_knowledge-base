---
title: Protecting Album Covers
date: 2026-07-25
---
___

For many collectors, the album jacket is just as important as the record itself. Original artwork, lyric sheets, and inserts contribute to both the visual appeal and collectible value of an album. Damaged covers may lower the value of a record, even if the vinyl remains in excellent condition.

Protective outer sleeves made from polyethylene or polypropylene help shield album covers from scratches, dirt, and moisture. Collectors should avoid writing on covers, placing heavy objects on top of records, or exposing albums to direct sunlight, which can cause fading.

If a record includes posters, lyric sheets, or other inserts, they should remain inside the jacket to prevent them from being lost. Taking a few extra steps to protect packaging helps preserve both the historical and monetary value of a collection.
