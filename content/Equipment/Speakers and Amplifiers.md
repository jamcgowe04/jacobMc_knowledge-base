---
title: Speakers and Amplifiers
date: 2026-07-25
---
___

Unlike digital devices that often contain built in speakers, most turntables contain additional audio equipment that is required to produce sound. An amplifier increases the tiny electrical signal generated by the cartridge, while speakers convert that signal into audible music.

Some turntables include a built-in phono preamp, allowing them to connect directly to powered speakers. Others require an external phono preamp before connecting to an amplifier. Understanding this difference is important when purchasing equipment because incompatible components may prevent a system from functioning correctly. 

Speaker Placement also plays a significant role in sound quality. Positioning speakers on sturdy surfaces and away from the turntable can reduce unwanted vibrations and improve the listening experiences.
