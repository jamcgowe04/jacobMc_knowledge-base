___

The cartridge is mounted on the end of the tonearm and holds the stylus, commonly referred to as the needle. As the stylus travels through the grooves of a vinyl record, it converts tiny vibrations into electrical signals that become the music hear through speakers.

The Stylus is one of the most delicate components in a vinyl playback system. A worn or damaged stylus can permanently scratch records, making routine inspection and replacement an important part of record ownership. Most manufacturers recommend replacing the stylus after several hundred hours of playback, although this varies depending on its design and how well it has been maintained.

Collectors often choose between moving magnet (MM) and moving coil (MC) cartridges. Moving magnet cartridges are more affordable, easier to replace, and are commonly found on entry-level systems. Moving coil cartridges generally provide greater detail but require specialized equipment and are typically used in higher-end audio systems.
