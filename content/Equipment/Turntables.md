# Turntables

The turntable is the center of any vinyl record setup. Its spins the record at a constant speed while allowing the stylus to read the grooves etched into the vinyl. Without a properly functioning turntable, records are unable to play accurately, and poor-quality equipment may even damage a collection over time.

Modern turntables are available in two primary drive systems: belt-drive and direct-drive. Belt-Drive turntables use a rubber belt to rotate the platter, reducing motor vibrations and making them popular among home listeners seeking accurate sound reproduction. Direct-drive turntables connect motor directly to the platter, providing quick start-up times and consistent speed, making them a favorite among DJs

Many beginner turntables include automatic features that raise and lower the tonearm for the listener, while more advanced models are fully manual and offer greater control over playback. When purchasing a turntable, collectors should consider build quality, speed accuracy, adjustable tracking force, and the ability to upgrade components such as the cartridge.
