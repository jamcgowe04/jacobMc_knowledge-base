# Why I Started My Own Record Collection

### Earlier this year in March, I was given a record player for my birthday. I had talked about owning one before in the past and finally owning one was a dream come true.
### What inspired me to start a record collection was the idea of owning my favorite albums of music, but in a physical format. I believe it would also help fulfill my enjoyment and hobby of listening to music itself. For me, it sounds like a relaxing idea to just put on an album and let it play all the way through. I think it's also a good way to hear various artists differently and to really feel and imagine how they put their albums together, the process of recording each song, and how an album flows the way it does.

## First Record
### My first record was "Unbothered" by Lil Skies. This album is one of my favorite albums he ever recorded and is his second [[Glossary|LP]]. Initially, I was looking for his Mixtape "Life of a Dark Rose" but in my search I happen to stumble across this one instead. I found Lil Skies when I was in High School and I was able to connect some of his lyrics back to myself and what I was going through at the time. It also inspired my taste in music today.

## My Turntable
### The turntable I use to play my records is a Core Innovations Wireless Bluetooth turntable. It has 3 speeds, a built in speaker, with a clean wood finish and a clear cover that protects it from dust. This player was given to me as a birthday present and is okay for a starter turntable. Sooner or later I hope to upgrade to one that's more versatile and has better quality sound, but for now, this player does well for what it is.
