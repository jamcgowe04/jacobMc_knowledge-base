Record Collecting is a recent hobby I picked up that takes my appreciation for music, the history behind it and the idea of personal expression. While streaming music is more of a common occurrence in today's world, vinyl records provide a listening experience that is unique and emphasizes physical ownership, album artwork, and craftsmanship behind the recordings.

This section is an introduction to what record collecting is, its history, terminology, and how I started my own collection.
