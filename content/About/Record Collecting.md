# Record Collecting 
______
## What is Record Collecting?
### Record collecting is the hobby of preserving and acquiring sound recordings. Vinyl records have been around since the [[History|19th century]] and initially got there popularity from the invention of the phonograph and the gramophone. The big idea on why many people today still collect vinyl records is to appreciate the quality of sound, experience, artwork, and nostalgia that physical records still have. In recent years, there's been a huge resurgence in collecting records. This is being driven by young adults and mainstream music which has become a catalyst in earning sales by selling vinyl's. In what was once a nostalgic hobby has now transitioned into a large commercial success. Usually, records consist of music, but sometimes records can carry poetry, books and readings, speeches, or even ambience. Any type of formatting can be collected, but this Knowledge Base will focus on just music recordings. It also focuses on what I've found  in my search, [[Why I Started|Why I Started Record Collecting]], and what I've learned about owning a collection since I've started earlier this year.
